# Correction: one figure was mislabelled

Written 2026-10-19 02:36 IST, during the build, when the README numbers were
re-derived from the committed snapshot by `scripts/author_coverage.py`.

## What was wrong

`out/16_kill_test.md`, section 3, said:

> Of 761 merged RFCs, **83** were authored by someone who holds no current team
> membership and has no alumni record in any team file.

**That definition was wrong.** 83 was the subset of the 385 orphan RFCs that carried
`disposition-merge`, not a count of RFCs whose author has no membership and no alumni
record. Two different measures shared one label, which is exactly the failure mode
this project keeps hitting.

## The corrected figures, each with its definition

Scope: the 761 merged RFC PRs in `rust-lang/rfcs` at the pinned revision, and all
224 team files in `rust-lang/team`. MEASURED 2026-10-19 by
`scripts/author_coverage.py`.

| measure | definition | value |
|---|---|---|
| author not on an active team | absent from every active team's leads/members | **381 of 761, 50.1%** |
| author in no team file at all | no people file, no alumni entry, active or archived | **169 of 761, 22.2%** |
| no current membership and no alumni record | union of the two conditions above | **169 of 761, 22.2%** |
| the same, restricted to `outcome == merge` | the decisions subset | **41 of 761, 5.4%** |

The earlier "385" was measured before the archived-team bug was found, when the
roster covered only depth-1 team files. On the corrected roster that figure is 381.
The earlier "83" is not a count of anything meaningful; the merge-labelled subset of
the never-on-a-team group is 41.

## Why the numbers moved between measurements, plainly

1. **The archived-team bug.** The first roster read only 172 active team files and
   missed 52 archived ones, so more authors looked like orphans than really were.
2. **A regex bug.** Empty `members = []` followed by `alumni = [...]` swallowed the
   alumni list, and inline tables had their role names counted as handles. Caught by
   an independent `tomllib` parser.
3. **The refresh.** A later re-fetch to current HEAD moved `libs-ping` membership by
   one, so member edges went 1051 to 1050.

## What is still true

The **gap report is unaffected**: `libs-api` carries 138 merged RFCs and `core` 24,
both archived, both with a recorded successor, totalling 162 RFCs that a
successor-blind router would drop. `T-dev-tools` (20) and `T-doc` (6) remain true
gaps. Those come from label counts, not from author counts, and they were re-verified
at 2026-10-19 02:20 IST against the refreshed snapshot.

## The rule this leaves behind

Every number gets a definition and a denominator in the same sentence, and the
sentence is generated by a script rather than typed. `scripts/author_coverage.py`
and `scripts/verify_readme_numbers.py` exist for that reason and are run before any
figure reaches the README.
